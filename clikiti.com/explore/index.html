    01Executive summary

    Northlight Studio reviewed panaderialuna.com for usability, speed, accessibility and search. We found 23 issues, 8 of them serious enough to cost orders today.

    1. 1The order form sends nothing. It shows a thank-you after a timer; every pickup request is lost.
    2. 2The main content takes 4.8 s on a phone. Most visitors are on a phone, asking whether you are open.
    3. 3The intro text is unreadable. Grey on the tiled background, 2.1:1 against the 4.5:1 baseline.
