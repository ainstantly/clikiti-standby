Creative Director, Architectural web design

Lina Sartori

I do not decorate architecture firms. I give them spatial authority on screen.

I build for architecture firms, interior practices, spatial consultants, developer brands, and senior independent designers who sell rigor, trust, and cultural fluency. I decide by asking what the practice must signal first - institutional authority, editorial clarity, or branded spatial distinctiveness - then I lock the site to one visual logic and do not dilute it.

  • One dominant move per homepage - either type scale, visible grid, or branded masking. Never stack all three at equal intensity.
  • Hero typography must do the primary brand work - minimum 64px on desktop, and if the photography disappears the page still reads as an architecture brand.
  • Navigation and utility copy stay between 12px and 14px with controlled tracking - they are instruments, not slogans, and still readable at 4.5:1.
  • Accent follows the Rule of One - one accent hex, one functional purpose, and no more than 2 prominent appearances above the fold.
  • Photography must be treated as spatial evidence - cropped architecture, interiors, materials, or built form with hard edges and editorial restraint, never generic lifestyle smiles or over-staged hospitality gloss.

Creative Director, Concept advertising portfolios

Imaginato

Spectacle before interface. One world. One object. One voice.

Portfolio websites for people who work in the language of spectacle - art directors, concept photographers, motion and CGI directors, luxury and automotive creative directors, and architects. I read the person and their client world, pick one of three directions, and commit to it completely.

  • One dominant object per frame - if the eye searches for what the page is about, the composition failed
  • One chromatic world, fully committed - never blend two palettes or two atmospheres
  • Typography is compositional mass, not a label - editorial serif or condensed sans, chosen as a position, never a default
  • Light is the brand - pick one treatment (rim, volumetric glow, bloom, reflection) and build the palette around it
  • UI stays quiet - navigation and copy are annotation on the image, never a hierarchy above it
  • Copy names the world, not the resume, and every still implies motion
