Clikiti and the alternatives

Website audit tools such as AuditPitch, Insites, MyWebAudit and SEOptimer tell a prospect what is wrong with their site; site builders such as Readdy, Framer and Relume build a new one. Clikiti is best for web design agencies and freelancers who pitch outbound and want to send each prospect an audit plus a redesign of their own homepage before the first call. Each comparison below says where the other tool is stronger, and when it is the better choice.

Direct alternatives

Audit, report and prospecting tools

These do part of the same job as Clikiti: they help an agency win a client before any work starts.

AI audit + outreach tool

Clikiti vs AuditPitch

AuditPitch is best for freelancers and small agencies who want cheap, high-volume audit-led cold email with built-in follow-ups.

Branded audit reports + lead widgets

Clikiti vs MyWebAudit

MyWebAudit is best for agencies that sell on detailed branded reports across many audit types, want inbound lead widgets and also report to existing clients monthly.

SEO audit + reporting suite

Clikiti vs SEOptimer

SEOptimer is best for SEO-led agencies that sell rankings and want branded SEO reports, rank tracking and an embeddable lead-gen audit.

SEO review + sales tools

Clikiti vs WooRank

WooRank is best for agencies and sales teams that pitch and deliver SEO and want unlimited branded SEO reviews plus rank tracking.

Embeddable SEO audit for inbound leads

Clikiti vs MySiteAuditor

MySiteAuditor is best for SEO consultants and agencies that want an audit form on their own site to collect inbound leads at a flat monthly price.

Audit platform for selling digital services

Clikiti vs Insites

Insites is best for agencies and larger sales teams that sell several digital services (SEO, local, ads, websites) and want one audit platform with prospect search, CRM sync and full white-label.

Lead lists by keyword and city

Clikiti vs D7 Lead Finder

D7 Lead Finder is best for agencies that run high-volume cold outreach and need large, cheap contact lists by niche and city.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AuditPitch alternative?

If you want to show prospects a redesign rather than only a written audit, Clikiti. If you mainly need SEO reports, SEOptimer or WooRank. If you want many audit types and inbound lead widgets, MyWebAudit. If you sell many digital services with a sales team, Insites.

How is Clikiti different from website audit tools?

Audit tools report on what is wrong with a site. Clikiti also finds prospects that fit your agency and rebuilds each prospect’s homepage in their own brand, so the audit and the fix arrive together in one branded link.

Should I use a site builder like Framer, Relume or Lovable to pitch prospects?

You can prompt a demo page in any of them, but you would still find the prospect, audit their site and carry their brand over by hand. Clikiti does those steps. For building the real site after the client signs, those builders are the better tools.

Is Clikiti a replacement for an SEO tool?

No. Clikiti does not track rankings, keywords or backlinks. Many agencies use an SEO tool for SEO work and Clikiti for design-led prospecting.
