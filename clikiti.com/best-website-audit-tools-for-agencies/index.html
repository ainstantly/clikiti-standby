Best website audit tools for agencies (2026)

SEOptimer and WooRank are best for SEO-led agencies that need rank tracking and white-label SEO reports. MyWebAudit is best for branded reports across many audit types and inbound lead widgets, and MySiteAuditor for a flat-price SEO audit form on your own site. Insites is best for teams selling many digital services who want prospect search and CRM sync. AuditPitch is best for low-cost, audit-led cold email.

Clikiti is best for web design agencies and freelancers who pitch outbound and want to send each prospect an audit plus a redesign of their own homepage before the first call.

Quick picks

Which one fits your agency

  • You sell SEO retainers: SEOptimer or WooRank. Both crawl sites, track keywords and produce branded SEO reports.
  • You want many audit types and inbound leads: MyWebAudit, with Google Business Profile, e-commerce and ROI audits plus embeddable lead widgets.
  • Your leads come from your own website: MySiteAuditor, an embeddable SEO audit form at a flat monthly price.
  • You sell many digital services with a sales team: Insites, with prospect search, broad audits, CRM sync and full white-label.
  • You run cold email on a tight budget: AuditPitch, which writes outreach and follow-ups from its findings.
  • You sell websites and want to show the fix: Clikiti, which finds prospects, audits usability, accessibility and speed, and rebuilds the homepage in the prospect’s brand.

1 · Clikiti

Clikiti: audit plus a redesign of the prospect’s homepage

Clikiti finds businesses that match your ideal client profile, then four scan agents (Whiskers, Shadow, Tiger and Paws) check usability against Nielsen’s heuristics, accessibility against WCAG 2.2 AA, speed and page weight, and how forms behave. It then rebuilds the prospect’s homepage in about a minute with their own brand and content and packages audit and redesign as one branded link you send yourself.

Where others are stronger: Clikiti is not an SEO suite (no rank tracking, keyword or backlink data), offers fewer audit types than MyWebAudit or Insites, and does not send email for you.

Pricing: Scan free; Starter $49/month (15 pitches); Growth $129/month (50 pitches); Agency $349/month (150 pitches).

2 · AuditPitch

AuditPitch: AI audit + outreach tool

AuditPitch is an AI website audit tool for agencies and freelancers. It scans a public URL for SEO, technical, design, UX, accessibility and content issues, then turns the findings into audit reports, personalized outreach drafts and follow-up sequences.

Strengths

  • Very low entry price, with a usable free plan.
  • Writes outreach emails and follow-up sequences from the audit findings, with open tracking and stop rules.
  • Bulk analysis from a CSV of URLs, emails and names.
  • Covers AI-search (answer engine) structure checks such as headings, schema and entity clarity.

Pricing: Free plan, then $10/month (Growth) and $50/month (Scale). Checked 6 November 2026.

Best for: Freelancers and small agencies who want cheap, high-volume audit-led cold email with built-in follow-ups.

3 · MyWebAudit

MyWebAudit: branded audit reports + lead widgets

MyWebAudit (My Web Audit) lets agencies run AI-powered audits and produce branded reports for prospects and clients: website, SEO, Google Business Profile, e-commerce, landing page, competitive, page speed, ROI and accessibility audits, plus monthly client reporting and embeddable lead widgets.

Strengths

  • The widest range of audit types of the tools here, including Google Business Profile, e-commerce, ROI and competitive audits.
  • Embeddable lead widgets that turn visitors on your own site into leads.
  • Monthly client reporting, so the same tool supports retention after the sale.
  • Reports in eight languages and in HTML, PDF or Google Docs formats, with your own audit points.
  • Integrations with CRM and automation tools such as HighLevel, Zapier and Mailchimp.

Pricing: Pro $69.99, Studio $129.99, Agency $199.99 per month billed monthly; annual billing works out cheaper per month. Checked 6 November 2026.

Best for: Agencies that sell on detailed branded reports across many audit types, want inbound lead widgets and also report to existing clients monthly.

4 · SEOptimer

SEOptimer: SEO audit + reporting suite

SEOptimer is an SEO audit and AI-visibility tool. It audits on-page and technical SEO, crawls sites, tracks keywords, monitors backlinks and produces white-label PDF reports, with an embeddable audit tool for lead generation.

Strengths

  • Much deeper SEO: site crawler, keyword research and rank tracking, backlink monitoring.
  • White-label PDF reports in many languages, with scheduled reports.
  • Embeddable audit tool (Max plan) that captures leads on your own site.
  • Separate GEO, local SEO and Google Business Profile audits on higher plans.
  • Low monthly price for an SEO suite, with an API.

Pricing: Starter $29, Pro $39, Max $59 per month (about 25% less billed annually); 14-day free trial. Checked 12 October 2026.

Best for: SEO-led agencies that sell rankings and want branded SEO reports, rank tracking and an embeddable lead-gen audit.

5 · WooRank

WooRank: SEO review + sales tools

WooRank offers instant website reviews, technical site crawls, keyword research and tracking, competitor monitoring and white-label PDF reports, plus a lead generation tool, bulk reporting and an API for agencies and sales teams.

Strengths

  • Long-established SEO review tool with technical site crawls across thousands of pages.
  • Keyword tracking and competitor monitoring for ongoing client work.
  • Unlimited website reviews and branded PDF reports on Premium.
  • Bulk reporting, a browser extension and an API for high-volume teams.

Pricing: Pro $89.99/month (1 project), Premium $199.99/month (5 projects, branded PDFs); yearly and Enterprise options. Checked 6 November 2026.

Best for: Agencies and sales teams that pitch and deliver SEO and want unlimited branded SEO reviews plus rank tracking.

6 · MySiteAuditor

MySiteAuditor: embeddable SEO audit for inbound leads

MySiteAuditor is a white-label SEO audit tool built around a form you embed on your own website. A visitor enters their site and contact details, gets a branded SEO report, and you get the lead by email. You can also run audits by hand and send the PDF.

Strengths

  • Leads come to you: the audit form on your own site turns visitors into contacts without any outreach.
  • A flat monthly price with unlimited reports.
  • Reports in more than 20 languages, sent by email, downloaded or printed as PDF.
  • Connects to Zapier and Mailchimp on Professional.

Pricing: Starter $49/month (manual reports) and Professional $79/month (embedded forms and automatic leads); annual billing gives two months free. No free plan is listed. Checked 6 November 2026.

Best for: SEO consultants and agencies that want an audit form on their own site to collect inbound leads at a flat monthly price.

7 · Insites

Insites: audit platform for selling digital services

Insites is an audit platform for people who sell digital services. It scans a business across AI search visibility, SEO, local SEO, performance, accessibility, ads and social, and turns the result into a branded report with plain-language fixes. It also finds businesses by industry and location, runs bulk audits, writes outreach emails from the findings and syncs with CRMs.

Strengths

  • Much wider audit coverage: AI search visibility, SEO, local SEO and Google Business Profile, performance, ads, social and technology, with more than 200 checks in total (its figure).
  • Prospect search by industry and location with audit scores attached, plus bulk audits of up to 10,000 businesses from a CSV.
  • Deep white-label: your own scan domain, sending address and login page, with Insites branding removed.
  • CRM sync with Salesforce, HubSpot and Zoho, an API and webhooks, and email open tracking.
  • Unlimited users, so it fits large sales teams as well as agencies.

Pricing: No public prices: a custom annual subscription quoted after a demo, priced by usage. No free tier or trial. Checked 6 November 2026.

Best for: Agencies and larger sales teams that sell several digital services (SEO, local, ads, websites) and want one audit platform with prospect search, CRM sync and full white-label.

Adjacent tools

Lead lists, site builders and the manual approach

These come up in the same searches but do a different job. D7 Lead Finder builds the list before the pitch; Readdy also sends outreach; the rest help you build the site once the client has signed. None lists an audit of a prospect’s existing site.

Lead lists by keyword and city

D7 Lead Finder

D7 Lead Finder builds lists of businesses from a keyword and a city, with email, phone, address, website, social links, review scores and a few website signals such as mobile-friendliness and whether the business runs ads. Lists export to CSV, Excel or PDF.

Pricing: Starter US$44.99, Agency US$54.99 and Professional US$119.99 per month including tax; limited free use (5 searches a day, no export).

Best for: Agencies that run high-volume cold outreach and need large, cheap contact lists by niche and city.

Clikiti vs D7 Lead Finder

Site builder with agency outreach

Readdy

Readdy is a website builder you use by chatting, with hosting, a backend, a shop and integrations. For agencies it adds Outreach: it generates one demo website per lead in a batch and sends a cold email campaign from your own mailbox. Higher plans add client workspaces, white-label and client billing.

Pricing: Free plan; Starter $25, Pro $40, Agency from $80 and Agency Pro (white label) from $199 a month, about 40% less on a yearly plan; Ultimate $1,299 a month.

Best for: Agencies that want one tool to mass-produce demo sites, send the cold emails and then build and host the client’s site.

Clikiti vs Readdy

App and website builder from chat

Lovable

Lovable turns plain-language requests into editable code. It builds web apps and websites with a built-in backend (database, sign-in, storage), hosting and Git sync, and it can import a public website from its URL as an editable project.

Pricing: Free plan (5 credits a day, up to 30 a month); Pro from $25 a month and Business from $50 a month for 100 credits, with larger credit tiers; two months free on yearly billing.

Best for: Building the real site or web app once the client has signed, especially when it needs a backend, sign-in or custom features.

Clikiti vs Lovable

Sitemap, wireframe and site builder

Relume

Relume builds marketing websites from a brief: a sitemap, then wireframes, then designs and a style guide, assembled from its own library of human-designed components. Sites export to Figma, Webflow or React, or can be hosted on Relume.

Pricing: Build with Relume: free (about one website a month) or Pro from $14 a month; hosting from $12 per project a month; Site Builder plans with Webflow and React export from $18 a month.

Best for: Designers and agencies planning and building the real site, from sitemap to Figma, Webflow or React, after the deal is won.

Clikiti vs Relume

Website builder with a design agent

Framer

Framer is a website builder with hosting, a CMS, analytics and SEO settings. Its design agent builds editable pages, sections, copy and visuals from a prompt, and an AI review checks your own site for contrast issues, typos, missing alt text and SEO gaps before you publish.

Pricing: Free; Basic $10 and Pro $30 a month per site as shown with yearly billing; extra editors $20 a month; Enterprise custom.

Best for: Designing, publishing and hosting the client’s real site after they sign, especially for design-led teams.

Clikiti vs Framer

Design tool, and the hand-made spec pitch

Figma

Figma is a design platform. Agencies use it to mock up spec redesigns for prospects by hand. Its newer tools add AI: Figma Make turns prompts into prototypes and web apps and can pull in the content of an existing website from its URL, and Figma Sites (beta) publishes websites from Figma designs.

Pricing: Free Starter plan; Professional Full seat $20 a month ($16 billed yearly); Organization $55 and Enterprise $90 per Full seat a month, billed yearly.

Best for: A few high-value prospects where a bespoke concept from your own designer is worth the time, and all the design work after the client signs.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the best website audit tool for agencies in 2026?

It depends on what you sell. For SEO retainers, SEOptimer or WooRank give the deepest SEO data and white-label SEO reports. For branded reports across many audit types plus inbound lead widgets, MyWebAudit; for an SEO audit form on your own site at a flat price, MySiteAuditor. For a team selling many digital services with CRM sync, Insites. For low-cost audit-led cold email with follow-ups, AuditPitch. For design-led outbound, where you want to show a prospect a redesign of their own homepage next to the audit, Clikiti.

Which website audit tool also creates a redesign?

Of the audit tools compared here, only Clikiti rebuilds the prospect’s homepage in their own brand as part of the audit. The others deliver written or PDF reports; Insites can make a demo site through its Duda integration. Site builders such as Readdy, Relume and Framer create new sites, but none lists an audit of a prospect’s existing site.

Which audit tool is cheapest for agencies?

AuditPitch has the lowest published paid price ($10/month for Growth, as checked) and a free plan. SEOptimer starts at $29/month. Clikiti’s free scan costs nothing, and paid plans start higher because each pitch includes a generated redesign.

Do I need a white-label audit tool?

If you send audits to prospects under your agency’s name, yes. AuditPitch (paid plans), MyWebAudit, Insites, MySiteAuditor, SEOptimer (Pro and Max), WooRank (Premium) and Clikiti (Growth and Agency) all offer branded or white-label output; the details differ, see the table.

How long does a manual audit and redesign pitch take?

Often a day or more of a designer’s time per prospect for a Figma spec redesign plus a written audit. Clikiti produces the audit and redesign in about a minute, from about $2.33 per pitch.
