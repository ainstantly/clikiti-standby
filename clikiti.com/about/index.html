The company

Clikiti is a product of Ainstantly.The same idea, for a different customer.

It turns WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger into a sales, support and collections engine: from the first message to a verified payment, answered automatically and in Spanish, with every payment receipt checked for fraud before an order ships.

Our mission

Give every small agency the output of a full studio.Starting with the pitch.

Winning web work means showing up with proof. A careful audit and a redesign take a designer a day for each prospect, so most pitches go out as a generic email and a price list, and most of them go unanswered.

Clikiti does that day of work before the first call. The prospect opens a report with your name on it and their new website inside, and you see the moment they do.

  1. 01Find the prospect

    Businesses whose website is costing them customers, found and qualified for you.

  2. 02Audit with evidence

    Usability, accessibility, speed and search, with the page element behind every finding.

  3. 03Rebuild the homepage

    A finished redesign built from the client’s own facts, not a template.

  4. 04Pitch and follow up

    A report and a proposal under your name, and you see every time they open it.
