1. Font replacer

Every font, swapped.

Swap every font in a frame, a page or the whole file. Each weight maps to its nearest match, text styles update, and text that now takes more lines is flagged. Missing fonts are fixed in one go.

  • Regular to Regular, SemiBold to Semi Bold: no weight left as a missing font.
  • One font for everything, weights kept, in one tick.
  • Replace on a copy, or in place with Cmd+Z to undo.
  • Free, with no sign-in.
2. Suggested font pairs

A pair for this page.

Pairs for your page from Clikiti’s curated pairs, each with the reasons it works: the contrast between them, the mood, and the weights you already use.

  • Keeps your body font when it reads well, and adds a heading face.
  • Says why: a grotesk on top, a reading serif below.
  • Says who it suits: education, media, culture.
3. Restyle

New look. Same words.

Twelve designed looks, or your own colours and fonts. Every colour gets a new one for the job it does, so text stays readable and the words never change. The original frame is never touched.

  • Text, logos and art are checked on the new ground before the frame is made.
  • Pick up to three looks and compare them side by side.
  • Or put them on one frame as variable modes and switch between them (5 free tries).

One frame, every look.Switch it in Figma’s right panel.

Put the looks you like on one frame as variable modes. The layers stay the same; only the mode changes, so you can compare in place and switch back in one click.

Your brand colour, or the look’s.One switch, on every look.

Each look brings its own highlight. Switch to your brand colour and every look keeps it, as bright as your page already uses it, with labels on it still readable.

4. Dark mode

Dark, and still readable.

A dark mode of your own page, from its own colours, or one of 12 dark styles, most of them not black. Cards sit a level lighter than the page, so the levels stay in order, and every text pair clears 4.5:1.

  • The hues are kept; the highlight moves only as far as it must.
  • Free, with no sign-in. A dark page gets a Light mode the same way.
  • Light and Dark as variable modes on one frame, one click back (switchable-frame tries).

Twelve dark styles.Spread round the colour wheel.

Dim, Graphite, Midnight, Indigo, Plum, Oxblood, Cocoa, Olive, Forest, Petrol, Brand-tinted, and True black for OLED screens. Each brings its own highlight: amber on navy, coral on indigo, mint on forest. Or keep your brand colour on all of them.

5. Generate palette

A pair that passes.

A new light and dark palette from UI rules: one highlight hue, neutrals tinted toward it, cards a step from the page, and every text pair at 4.5:1 or more.

  • Made for the job each colour does, not picked from a wheel.
  • Comes as a light and dark pair, ready for variable modes.
  • Free, with no sign-in. Each press adds a new light and dark pair to the looks.
6. Page Sense

It reads the page first.

Page Sense finds the nav, hero, features, logos, testimonials, pricing, FAQ, form, call to action and footer, then restyles each section for its job: a band behind the services, a strong colour behind the call to action, a dark footer.

  • It lists the sections it read; tap one to correct it, and a dashed one is a guess.
  • Standard recolours by what each colour does; Page Sense by what each section does.
  • 5 free tries, then a free Clikiti account.
7. Scan

A score, and the fixes.

A quick design-quality check of the selected frame, the way a careful reviewer would read it: text people can read, a phone version, tap targets, type, placeholder words and how ready the layers are to hand off.

  • Each fix names the layers and the number: icons at 1.00:1 that need 3:1, a 20 × 0 px tap target.
  • Pin the findings as notes on the canvas.
  • Free, with no sign-in. Nothing leaves your computer.
  • A deep scan of the rendered page is coming, with a free account.
8. Build, with a Clikiti account

Your frame, as a website.

Build turns the frame into a real web page: headings, links, a form that sends, and a phone layout. It is checked before launch, lands in your Clikiti workspace next to your clients, and opens as a page or downloads as a ZIP.

  • Sign in once; the plugin keeps its key on your computer.
  • Import brings a Clikiti page into Figma as editable layers, at 1440 px and 375 px.

Questions

Is the font replacer free?

Yes, permanently and guaranteed, without signing in. Swap fonts in a frame, a page or the whole file, keep every weight, update text styles and fix missing fonts in one go.

How do I replace every font in a Figma file?

Open the plugin, go to Restyle and then Fonts, pick All pages and choose the new font for each family, or tick One font for everything. Each weight maps to its nearest match, and text that now takes more lines is flagged.

Can it make a dark mode of my Figma design?

Yes. It makes a dark version of your own page from its colours, or one of 12 dark styles, most of them not black. Every text pair is checked to 4.5:1, and it is free with no sign-in. Light and Dark can also sit as variable modes on one frame, so you can switch back; that uses the switchable-frames tries.

Does it change my original frame?

No. Restyles and dark modes are made on a copy. Only Replace in this frame edits in place, when you press it, and Cmd+Z undoes it.

What does a free try count?

Page Sense, suggested pairs and switchable frames have 5 free tries each. A try is counted only when it changed something. After that, a free Clikiti account removes the limit.
