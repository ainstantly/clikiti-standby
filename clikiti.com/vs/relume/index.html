Relume and Clikiti

Clikiti vs Relume

Relume and Clikiti come in at different moments of the same project. Clikiti is for before the client signs: it finds the prospect, audits their site and shows them their homepage redesigned. Relume is for after: it plans the sitemap, wireframes every page and exports the design to Figma, Webflow or React.

If you already have the client, Relume is the better choice and Clikiti adds little. If you need the client first, Relume does not list site audits or prospect search, and Clikiti is built for that step.

Where each fits

Before the contract, and after it

Relume’s flow starts from a brief: a sitemap, then wireframes, then designs and a style guide built from its library of more than 1,000 human-designed components. That is project work, and it assumes someone has agreed to pay for a website.

Clikiti’s flow starts from a business you would like to work with. It scans the current site and rebuilds the homepage so the owner can see the difference before any meeting.

The existing site

What happens to the old website

Relume can import an existing site through its sitemap, up to 50 pages, and use the page titles and descriptions as prompts for new sections. Its help article does not say the page content itself is carried over; the new design comes from Relume’s components.

Clikiti keeps the prospect’s logo, colours, copy and real business facts and changes how the page is built and laid out. The aim is recognition: the owner sees their own business, improved.

Output

A pitch link, or production files

Relume exports a native Figma file with mobile variants, a Webflow project with its classes intact, or React code, and it can host the site with a CMS and SEO controls.

Clikiti’s output is a branded link holding the audit and the redesigned homepage, made to be sent to a prospect who has not hired you yet.

Pricing

Relume pricing vs Clikiti pricing

Build with Relume is free for about one website a month and starts from $14 a month on Pro; export to Webflow and React needs a Site Builder plan from $18 a month. Clikiti prices by pitches a month. The two costs pay for different stages of the work.

Fair verdict

Where Relume is stronger, and where Clikiti differs

Choose Relume if you want

  • It plans the whole site, from sitemap to wireframe to design.
  • It exports production-ready work to Figma, Webflow and React.
  • More than 1,000 human-designed components to build from.
  • It can pull the page list of an existing site from its sitemap, up to 50 pages.
  • Unlimited editors with no per-seat charge on Build with Relume.

Choose Clikiti if you want

  • Clikiti audits the prospect’s live site and keeps their brand and content in the redesign.
  • Clikiti finds prospects and packages audit and redesign as a branded pitch link.
  • Clikiti is used before the client signs; Relume fits the project after.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Clikiti a Relume alternative?

No, they do different jobs. Relume plans and builds a site from sitemap to export. Clikiti audits a prospect’s current site and pitches them a redesign of their homepage. An agency might use Clikiti to win the project and Relume to build it.

Does Relume audit websites?

Not that its site lists. It can import an existing site’s page list from its sitemap, but we found no usability, accessibility or speed audit (checked 6 November 2026).

What is the main difference between Clikiti and Relume?

Relume: Relume builds marketing websites from a brief: a sitemap, then wireframes, then designs and a style guide, assembled from its own library of human-designed components. Sites export to Figma, Webflow or React, or can be hosted on Relume. Clikiti finds prospects, scans their site for usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms issues, rebuilds their homepage with their own brand, and packages audit and redesign as one branded link.

How much does Clikiti cost?

Scan free; Starter $49/month (15 pitches); Growth $129/month (50 pitches); Agency $349/month (150 pitches). Prices in USD per month.
