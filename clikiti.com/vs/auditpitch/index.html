Clikiti vs AuditPitch

AuditPitch is best for freelancers and small agencies who want cheap, high-volume audit-led cold email with built-in follow-ups. Clikiti is best for web design agencies and freelancers who pitch outbound and want to send each prospect an audit plus a redesign of their own homepage before the first call.

The core difference: AuditPitch reports on what is wrong with a prospect’s website; Clikiti also shows the fix, a redesigned homepage in the prospect’s own brand.

Audit depth

What each tool audits

Both tools audit a public URL without access to the prospect’s backend. AuditPitch casts a wide net across SEO, technical, design, content and AI-search structure. Clikiti goes deeper on how real visitors experience the site: four agents check usability against Nielsen’s heuristics, accessibility against WCAG 2.2 AA, speed and page weight, and how forms behave under clumsy or repeated input.

Redesign

Describing the problem vs showing the fix

This is the biggest difference. AuditPitch suggests redesign angles in writing. Clikiti rebuilds the prospect’s homepage in about a minute, keeping their logo, colours, copy and real business facts, and fixing the issues the audit found. The prospect sees their own business, improved.

Prospecting

Where the prospects come from

AuditPitch works from URLs you bring, with CSV bulk import and a Lead Finder page. Clikiti also finds businesses for you that match your ideal client profile by niche, city and size, then scans them.

White-label

Whose brand the prospect sees

AuditPitch lists custom branding as a paid-plan feature. Clikiti’s Starter plan carries your branding with a small “Made with Clikiti” line; Growth and Agency remove it and let you serve reports from your own domain.

Pricing

AuditPitch pricing vs Clikiti pricing

AuditPitch is cheaper per audit. Clikiti costs more because each pitch includes a generated homepage redesign as well as a report. If you only need written findings and email sequences, AuditPitch is the more economical choice.

Fair verdict

Where AuditPitch is stronger, and where Clikiti differs

Choose AuditPitch if you want

  • Very low entry price, with a usable free plan.
  • Writes outreach emails and follow-up sequences from the audit findings, with open tracking and stop rules.
  • Bulk analysis from a CSV of URLs, emails and names.
  • Covers AI-search (answer engine) structure checks such as headings, schema and entity clarity.

Choose Clikiti if you want

  • Clikiti rebuilds the prospect’s homepage with their own brand and content, so the pitch shows the fix instead of describing it.
  • Clikiti’s audit is built around usability (Nielsen’s ten heuristics), WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms QA.
  • Clikiti does not send email for you: you send one branded link from your own inbox, LinkedIn or phone.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Clikiti an AuditPitch alternative?

Yes, for agencies that want to show a redesign rather than describe problems. Both audit a prospect’s site before outreach. AuditPitch focuses on audit reports plus automated email follow-ups; Clikiti adds prospect discovery and a full homepage redesign in the prospect’s own brand, packaged as one link.

Does Clikiti send cold emails like AuditPitch?

No. Clikiti never contacts prospects for you. On Growth and Agency it writes an outreach kit (email, LinkedIn message, call script) that you send yourself.

Which is cheaper, AuditPitch or Clikiti?

AuditPitch. Its paid plans start at $10/month (as checked on its pricing section). Clikiti’s plans cost more because every pitch includes a generated homepage redesign.

What is the main difference between Clikiti and AuditPitch?

AuditPitch: AuditPitch is an AI website audit tool for agencies and freelancers. It scans a public URL for SEO, technical, design, UX, accessibility and content issues, then turns the findings into audit reports, personalized outreach drafts and follow-up sequences. Clikiti finds prospects, scans their site for usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms issues, rebuilds their homepage with their own brand, and packages audit and redesign as one branded link.

How much does Clikiti cost?

Scan free; Starter $49/month (15 pitches); Growth $129/month (50 pitches); Agency $349/month (150 pitches). Prices in USD per month.
