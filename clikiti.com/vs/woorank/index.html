Clikiti vs WooRank

WooRank is best for agencies and sales teams that pitch and deliver SEO and want unlimited branded SEO reviews plus rank tracking. Clikiti is best for web design agencies and freelancers who pitch outbound and want to send each prospect an audit plus a redesign of their own homepage before the first call.

The core difference: WooRank reports on what is wrong with a prospect’s website; Clikiti also shows the fix, a redesigned homepage in the prospect’s own brand.

Audit depth

What each tool audits

WooRank reviews a site for SEO and can crawl thousands of pages for technical issues; it also links a free accessibility check run by a partner. Clikiti’s own scan covers usability heuristics, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms QA, written for a business owner rather than an SEO specialist.

Redesign

Describing the problem vs showing the fix

WooRank does not list a redesign feature. Clikiti rebuilds the prospect’s homepage in their brand, fixing what the audit found, and puts it next to the audit in one link.

Prospecting

Where the prospects come from

WooRank’s lead generation tool helps you check a prospect’s SEO health. Clikiti searches for prospects that match your ideal client profile and prepares a full pitch for each.

White-label

Whose brand the prospect sees

WooRank includes branded PDF reports on Premium. Clikiti is fully white-label from Growth, including your own report domain.

Fair verdict

Where WooRank is stronger, and where Clikiti differs

Choose WooRank if you want

  • Long-established SEO review tool with technical site crawls across thousands of pages.
  • Keyword tracking and competitor monitoring for ongoing client work.
  • Unlimited website reviews and branded PDF reports on Premium.
  • Bulk reporting, a browser extension and an API for high-volume teams.

Choose Clikiti if you want

  • Clikiti rebuilds the prospect’s homepage; WooRank reports on the existing site.
  • Clikiti’s audit centres on usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms rather than SEO.
  • Clikiti finds prospects by niche, city and size and packages each pitch as one link.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Clikiti a WooRank alternative?

For design-led prospecting, yes. For SEO work, WooRank is stronger: it has site crawls, keyword tracking and competitor monitoring. Clikiti audits usability, accessibility and speed, then sends the prospect a redesign of their own homepage.

Does Clikiti produce PDF reports like WooRank?

Clikiti delivers the audit and redesign as a branded web link rather than a PDF, so the prospect can click through the redesign.

What is the main difference between Clikiti and WooRank?

WooRank: WooRank offers instant website reviews, technical site crawls, keyword research and tracking, competitor monitoring and white-label PDF reports, plus a lead generation tool, bulk reporting and an API for agencies and sales teams. Clikiti finds prospects, scans their site for usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms issues, rebuilds their homepage with their own brand, and packages audit and redesign as one branded link.

How much does Clikiti cost?

Scan free; Starter $49/month (15 pitches); Growth $129/month (50 pitches); Agency $349/month (150 pitches). Prices in USD per month.
