Clikiti vs MySiteAuditor

MySiteAuditor and Clikiti sit at opposite ends of the funnel. MySiteAuditor waits on your website: a visitor types in their URL, gets a branded SEO report, and you get their details. Clikiti goes out: it finds businesses that fit your ideal client profile and prepares a pitch for each one, with an audit and a redesign of their own homepage.

If your leads already come from your own site and you sell SEO, MySiteAuditor is the cheaper and simpler choice. If you sell websites and need conversations with businesses that have never heard of you, Clikiti is built for that.

Inbound or outbound

Who starts the conversation

MySiteAuditor’s main feature is the embedded audit form. It works when people already visit your site: the free report is the reason they leave their email. Its homepage counts 1,520,968 leads generated so far across its customers (the vendor’s own figure).

Clikiti starts from your side. You describe the clients you want by niche, city and size; Clikiti finds matching businesses, scans their sites and builds the pitch. The prospect sees it for the first time when you send the link.

Audit depth

One keyword, or the whole visit

A MySiteAuditor report scores one page for one target keyword against more than 50 SEO signals, and its crawl looks at up to 10 pages per domain. It has added checks for AI crawler access in robots.txt and for an llms.txt file.

Clikiti does not score keywords. Its scan checks usability against Nielsen’s ten heuristics, accessibility against WCAG 2.2 AA, speed and page weight, and how forms behave. That suits a pitch for a new website; MySiteAuditor’s report suits a pitch for SEO work.

What the prospect receives

A PDF report, or a link with their new homepage

MySiteAuditor reports go out by email, as a download or as a printable PDF, in your branding and in more than 20 languages.

Clikiti sends one branded link. It holds the audit and a rebuilt homepage that keeps the prospect’s logo, colours, copy and business facts, with the problems from the audit fixed. MySiteAuditor lists no redesign or mockup feature.

Pricing

MySiteAuditor pricing vs Clikiti pricing

MySiteAuditor charges a flat fee for unlimited reports. Clikiti charges by the number of redesign pitches a month. For high-volume inbound SEO reports MySiteAuditor costs less; Clikiti’s price pays for a generated redesign in every pitch.

Where MySiteAuditor is stronger, and where Clikiti differs

Choose MySiteAuditor if you want

  • Leads come to you: the audit form on your own site turns visitors into contacts without any outreach.
  • A flat monthly price with unlimited reports.
  • Reports in more than 20 languages, sent by email, downloaded or printed as PDF.
  • Connects to Zapier and Mailchimp on Professional.

Choose Clikiti if you want

  • Clikiti is for outbound: it finds businesses that fit your ideal client profile instead of waiting for visitors to fill in a form.
  • Clikiti checks how the site works for visitors (usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed, forms), where MySiteAuditor scores one page against one keyword.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Clikiti a MySiteAuditor alternative?

Only if you want outbound prospecting. MySiteAuditor is an inbound lead form for SEO audits on your own website. Clikiti finds prospects for you and sends each one an audit and a redesign of their homepage. An agency could run both: MySiteAuditor on its own site, Clikiti for outreach.

Does MySiteAuditor have a free plan?

No free plan is listed on its pricing page (checked 6 November 2026). Plans start at $49/month, and both carry a 14-day satisfaction guarantee.

What is the main difference between Clikiti and MySiteAuditor?

MySiteAuditor: MySiteAuditor is a white-label SEO audit tool built around a form you embed on your own website. A visitor enters their site and contact details, gets a branded SEO report, and you get the lead by email. You can also run audits by hand and send the PDF. Clikiti finds prospects, scans their site for usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms issues, rebuilds their homepage with their own brand, and packages audit and redesign as one branded link.

How much does Clikiti cost?

Scan free; Starter $49/month (15 pitches); Growth $129/month (50 pitches); Agency $349/month (150 pitches). Prices in USD per month.
