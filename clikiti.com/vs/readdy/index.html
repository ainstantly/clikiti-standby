Clikiti vs Readdy

Readdy is a website builder that also does agency outreach: from a lead list it generates one demo website per business and emails each a link from your own mailbox. Clikiti is narrower. It audits the prospect’s current website, rebuilds that homepage with their own brand and content, and leaves the sending to you.

Choose Readdy if you want volume and automation in one place and plan to build and host the client’s site in the same tool. Choose Clikiti if your pitch rests on the prospect’s own site: what is wrong with it, and what it looks like fixed.

Starting point

A row in a list, or the prospect’s live site

Readdy’s Outreach docs say each row of your lead list generates one personalized website, at 5 credits a row, and the email links to it. Neither its feature page nor its docs say the generation reads or rebuilds the prospect’s existing website.

Clikiti starts from the website itself. It reads the prospect’s homepage, keeps their logo, colours, copy and real business facts, and fixes what the audit found, so the owner recognises their own business.

Audit

The reason to reply

Readdy lists an SEO analysis for sites you build in Readdy, covering SEO, performance, security and content checks. It lists no audit of a prospect’s live site and no audit report to send.

Every Clikiti pitch includes a scan of the live site: usability against Nielsen’s ten heuristics, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and page weight, and forms. The audit tells the owner why the redesign matters.

Sending

Who presses send

Readdy sends the campaign. You connect Gmail or any SMTP mailbox, set the dates, sending window and days, switch on warm-up per mailbox if you want it, and watch opens, clicks and replies. Its docs recommend 20 to 30 emails per mailbox per day.

Clikiti sends nothing. It gives you one branded link and, from Growth, an email, a LinkedIn message and a call script to adapt. That is slower per prospect, and every message goes out in your own words from your own account.

After the sale

Building the real site

Readdy builds, hosts and runs the client’s site: backend, forms, booking, a shop and payments, custom domains, code export and GitHub sync, with a white-label client platform on Agency Pro. If you want one subscription for the pitch and the build, it covers more ground than Clikiti, which is built around the pitch.

Pricing

Readdy pricing vs Clikiti pricing

Readdy prices by credits and site slots, and its pricing page lists Outreach from the Agency plan at $80 a month. Its docs give Outreach quotas for every plan, including Free, so check which applies before you buy. Clikiti prices by redesign pitches a month.

Fair verdict

Where Readdy is stronger, and where Clikiti differs

Choose Readdy if you want

  • It sends the cold email for you: connect your own mailbox, set a sending window, turn on warm-up, and track opens, clicks and replies.
  • It generates a demo site per lead in batches, from a lead list or an uploaded spreadsheet.
  • It builds, hosts and runs the real site after the sale, with forms, booking, a shop, payments and custom domains.
  • Client workspaces and, on Agency Pro, a white-label client platform with client billing.

Choose Clikiti if you want

  • Clikiti starts from the prospect’s current website: it audits it and rebuilds that homepage with their own logo, colours and copy.
  • Every Clikiti pitch carries an audit of the live site (usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed, forms). Readdy lists no audit of a prospect’s site.
  • Clikiti never sends email for you; you send one branded link from your own inbox, LinkedIn or phone.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Clikiti a Readdy alternative?

For the pitch, yes; for building and hosting sites, no. Readdy generates a demo site per lead and sends the emails, then builds and hosts the client’s site. Clikiti audits each prospect’s current site and rebuilds their homepage in their own brand for you to send.

Does Readdy redesign a prospect’s existing website?

Its Outreach docs say each lead row generates one personalized website. We found no feature page or doc saying Outreach starts from the prospect’s current site (checked 6 November 2026).

What is the main difference between Clikiti and Readdy?

Readdy: Readdy is a website builder you use by chatting, with hosting, a backend, a shop and integrations. For agencies it adds Outreach: it generates one demo website per lead in a batch and sends a cold email campaign from your own mailbox. Higher plans add client workspaces, white-label and client billing. Clikiti finds prospects, scans their site for usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms issues, rebuilds their homepage with their own brand, and packages audit and redesign as one branded link.

How much does Clikiti cost?

Scan free; Starter $49/month (15 pitches); Growth $129/month (50 pitches); Agency $349/month (150 pitches). Prices in USD per month.
