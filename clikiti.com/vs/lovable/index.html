Lovable for web design agencies

Clikiti vs Lovable

Lovable is not a like-for-like competitor. It is a builder: you describe what you want and it writes the code, with a database, sign-in and hosting included. Some agencies use it to make a quick demo site for a prospect, and that is where it meets Clikiti.

For that one job, Clikiti does more of the work around the demo: it finds the prospect, audits their current site and packages audit and redesign as a branded link. For everything after the client signs, from a full website to a booking system, Lovable is the better tool.

Different jobs

A pitching tool and a builder

Lovable’s docs list what it builds: company websites, landing pages, portfolios, dashboards, internal tools and SaaS products. It is a general builder priced by credits; its docs give examples from about half a credit to 1.7 credits per edit.

Clikiti does one thing: it prepares a pitch for a business that has not hired you yet. Finding the business, scanning its site and rebuilding the homepage are one flow, counted as one pitch.

Making the demo

Copying a site, or rebuilding it for a pitch

Lovable can import a public website from its URL. It reads text, images, fonts, colours and layout at desktop and phone widths, up to 10 pages per request, and makes an editable copy; its docs note that form handling is not copied. From there you prompt the changes yourself.

Clikiti rebuilds the homepage in one pass, keeping the prospect’s brand and content and changing what its scan found wrong, so each change has a reason the owner can read. You get one page, made to be sent.

Audit

Telling the prospect why

Lovable reviews SEO and AI-search readiness for your own project, and its docs say this review is not a full accessibility audit. It lists no way to audit someone else’s site.

Clikiti scans the prospect’s live site for usability against Nielsen’s ten heuristics, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and page weight, and forms. That report is what turns a nicer page into a pitch.

Pricing

Lovable pricing vs Clikiti pricing

Lovable charges by credits, from $25 a month for 100 on Pro, so the cost of a demo depends on how many edits it takes. Clikiti charges for a fixed number of pitches a month, each with a scan and a redesign. An agency could pay for both: Clikiti to win the client, Lovable to build what was sold.

Fair verdict

Where Lovable is stronger, and where Clikiti differs

Choose Lovable if you want

  • It builds working products as well as pages: database, sign-in, storage, functions and hosting.
  • It imports up to 10 pages of a public website per request into an editable project.
  • You own the code: Git sync with GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket, and download on paid plans.
  • Team controls on Business and Enterprise, such as single sign-on and roles.

Choose Clikiti if you want

  • Clikiti audits the prospect’s site first (usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed, forms) and puts the findings next to the redesign.
  • Clikiti finds prospects by niche, city and size; Lovable starts from a prompt or a URL you bring.
  • Each Clikiti pitch is a branded link with your agency’s name on it, ready to send.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Lovable to pitch web design clients?

Yes, by importing or prompting a demo site and sending the link. You would still find the prospect, audit their site and write the pitch yourself. Clikiti does those steps and packages the audit with the redesign.

Is Clikiti a Lovable alternative?

Only for pitching. Lovable builds full websites and apps with a backend and hosting. Clikiti prepares the pitch that wins the project.

What is the main difference between Clikiti and Lovable?

Lovable: Lovable turns plain-language requests into editable code. It builds web apps and websites with a built-in backend (database, sign-in, storage), hosting and Git sync, and it can import a public website from its URL as an editable project. Clikiti finds prospects, scans their site for usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms issues, rebuilds their homepage with their own brand, and packages audit and redesign as one branded link.

How much does Clikiti cost?

Scan free; Starter $49/month (15 pitches); Growth $129/month (50 pitches); Agency $349/month (150 pitches). Prices in USD per month.
