D7 Lead Finder alternative

Clikiti vs D7 Lead Finder

D7 Lead Finder and Clikiti both start from a niche and a city, then split. D7 Lead Finder returns hundreds of businesses per search with emails, phones and a few website signals, ready for a cold email tool. Clikiti returns a shorter list and does the work on each one: it scans the site and rebuilds the homepage, so you have something specific to send.

If your outreach is a numbers game and you write your own pitch, D7 Lead Finder is far cheaper per lead. If you want each message to show the prospect their own site, improved, Clikiti does that. The two can work together: build the list in D7 Lead Finder, then run a Clikiti scan on the businesses worth a real pitch.

Prospecting

Thousands of rows, or a short list with a pitch each

D7 Lead Finder returns up to 1,200 businesses per search, usually in three minutes or less by its own account, and its top plan allows about 30,000 leads a day. It also detects whether a business runs ads or uses tracking pixels, which can hint at a marketing budget.

Clikiti finds businesses that match your ideal client profile by niche, city and size, then spends its effort on each one. Every prospect gets a scan and a redesigned homepage, so the monthly limit is counted in pitches, not rows.

Website checks

Filters for a list, or findings for the owner

D7 Lead Finder’s website data is a set of yes-or-no signals: mobile-friendly, Google Analytics, schema, WordPress, email provider. They help you filter a list; they are not something to show a prospect.

Clikiti’s scan is written for the business owner: usability problems against Nielsen’s ten heuristics, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and page weight, and forms that fail. The redesign then fixes what the scan found.

Outreach

What you end up sending

D7 Lead Finder ends at the export: CSV, Excel or PDF, which you load into your own email or calling tools. It lists no outreach features.

Clikiti ends with one branded link that holds the audit and the redesign, and on Growth and Agency an outreach kit with an email, a LinkedIn message and a call script. You still send it yourself.

Pricing

D7 Lead Finder pricing vs Clikiti pricing

D7 Lead Finder is much cheaper per lead: its plans run from US$44.99 to US$119.99 a month including tax, for thousands of leads a day. Clikiti costs more per prospect because each one comes with a scan and a generated redesign. The fair comparison is cost per reply or per signed client, not per row.

Fair verdict

Where D7 Lead Finder is stronger, and where Clikiti differs

Choose D7 Lead Finder if you want

  • Volume: up to 1,200 businesses per search and tens of thousands of leads a day on the top plan.
  • Contact data ready to export: emails, phone numbers and social links.
  • Signals that hint at a buyer, such as whether a business runs ads or has a mobile-friendly site.
  • Works in many countries, with bulk search across up to 100 cities or keywords and an API.

Choose Clikiti if you want

  • Clikiti returns fewer businesses but prepares a full pitch for each: an audit and a redesign of their homepage.
  • Clikiti’s scan covers usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms; D7 Lead Finder reports a few signals, not an audit.
  • Clikiti gives you something to send. D7 Lead Finder gives you a list to work from.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Clikiti a D7 Lead Finder alternative?

Partly. Both find local businesses by niche and city. D7 Lead Finder gives you a large list with contact details to export; Clikiti gives you fewer prospects, each with an audit and a redesign of their homepage ready to send.

Does D7 Lead Finder audit websites?

Not in depth. It reports signals such as mobile-friendliness, analytics, schema and WordPress for each business. It does not list accessibility, usability or speed audits, or a report for the prospect.

What is the main difference between Clikiti and D7 Lead Finder?

D7 Lead Finder: D7 Lead Finder builds lists of businesses from a keyword and a city, with email, phone, address, website, social links, review scores and a few website signals such as mobile-friendliness and whether the business runs ads. Lists export to CSV, Excel or PDF. Clikiti finds prospects, scans their site for usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms issues, rebuilds their homepage with their own brand, and packages audit and redesign as one branded link.

How much does Clikiti cost?

Scan free; Starter $49/month (15 pitches); Growth $129/month (50 pitches); Agency $349/month (150 pitches). Prices in USD per month.
