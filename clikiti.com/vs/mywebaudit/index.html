Clikiti vs MyWebAudit

MyWebAudit is best for agencies that sell on detailed branded reports across many audit types, want inbound lead widgets and also report to existing clients monthly. Clikiti is best for web design agencies and freelancers who pitch outbound and want to send each prospect an audit plus a redesign of their own homepage before the first call.

The core difference: MyWebAudit reports on what is wrong with a prospect’s website; Clikiti also shows the fix, a redesigned homepage in the prospect’s own brand.

Audit depth

What each tool audits

MyWebAudit offers more audit types than Clikiti, including Google Business Profile, e-commerce, competitive and ROI audits, and lets you add your own audit points. Clikiti runs one combined scan focused on the visitor experience: usability heuristics, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms QA.

Redesign

Describing the problem vs showing the fix

MyWebAudit does not list a redesign or mockup feature. Clikiti’s core output is a rebuilt homepage that keeps the prospect’s brand and fixes what the audit found, delivered in the same link as the audit.

Prospecting

Where the prospects come from

MyWebAudit is built for inbound: lead widgets and lead magnets on your own site capture prospects who request an audit. Clikiti is built for outbound: it finds businesses that fit your ideal client profile and prepares a pitch for each.

White-label

Whose brand the prospect sees

MyWebAudit reports carry your branding, with a shared white-label domain on annual Pro and two custom white-label domains on Agency. Clikiti is fully white-label from Growth, including your own report domain.

Fair verdict

Where MyWebAudit is stronger, and where Clikiti differs

Choose MyWebAudit if you want

  • The widest range of audit types of the tools here, including Google Business Profile, e-commerce, ROI and competitive audits.
  • Embeddable lead widgets that turn visitors on your own site into leads.
  • Monthly client reporting, so the same tool supports retention after the sale.
  • Reports in eight languages and in HTML, PDF or Google Docs formats, with your own audit points.
  • Integrations with CRM and automation tools such as HighLevel, Zapier and Mailchimp.

Choose Clikiti if you want

  • Clikiti rebuilds the prospect’s homepage as part of the pitch; MyWebAudit delivers written audit reports.
  • Clikiti finds prospects that match your ideal client profile; MyWebAudit focuses on audits for prospects who come to you and for existing clients.
  • Clikiti is narrower: no Google Business Profile, ROI or monthly client reports.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Clikiti a MyWebAudit alternative?

For outbound pitching, yes. MyWebAudit is stronger for many audit types, inbound lead widgets and monthly client reporting. Clikiti is the better fit when you want to find prospects and send each one a redesign of their own homepage together with the audit.

Can I use MyWebAudit and Clikiti together?

Yes. Some agencies use an inbound audit widget on their own site and use Clikiti for outbound pitches to businesses they choose.

What is the main difference between Clikiti and MyWebAudit?

MyWebAudit: MyWebAudit (My Web Audit) lets agencies run AI-powered audits and produce branded reports for prospects and clients: website, SEO, Google Business Profile, e-commerce, landing page, competitive, page speed, ROI and accessibility audits, plus monthly client reporting and embeddable lead widgets. Clikiti finds prospects, scans their site for usability, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, speed and forms issues, rebuilds their homepage with their own brand, and packages audit and redesign as one branded link.

How much does Clikiti cost?

Scan free; Starter $49/month (15 pitches); Growth $129/month (50 pitches); Agency $349/month (150 pitches). Prices in USD per month.
