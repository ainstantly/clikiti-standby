Questions

Is the scan really free?

Yes. Paste any public homepage and you get the score, verdict, top findings and core metrics. No account, no card. The full findings list, the redesign and the sendable pitch are what you get with access.

What exactly does the free scan check?

Three things on the page you enter: usability against core Nielsen heuristics (consistency, error prevention, clear labels), the WCAG 2.2 AA checks a machine can test (colour contrast, alt text, form labels, link names, headings, page language) and speed signals such as page weight, request count, heavy assets, compression and server response. Deeper checks and a multi-page crawl come with the full scan in your workspace.

Does Clikiti contact prospects for me?

No. You stay in control of the relationship. Clikiti builds the audit and the redesign as one branded link, and you send it through your own inbox, LinkedIn or phone.

Who gets access?

Agencies and freelancers who actively pitch web work and will tell us what to fix. We review every request by hand during the alpha.

How long does a redesign take?

Clikiti Pages: 2 to 4 minutes. Signature pages take longer, because an agent designs them around one idea from the business.

Do the agents make things up?

No. The words keep the facts from the prospect’s own site, such as opening hours, prices and addresses, and every page is checked for lost facts before you see it.
