Ban a look and you get the next one.So every page starts with decisions.

When the industry dropped purple, it landed on cream, a serif and terracotta. A list of bans breeds the next default. So before anything is drawn, four things are decided from the business itself.

  1. One signatureA detail only this business has
  2. The coloursFrom the client’s real brand
  3. The typeTheir fonts, or a stated reason
  4. The layoutFollows the job of the page
The check

A rule, not a hope.

A deterministic check reads every page Clikiti makes. Blocking tells get one targeted fix; the fix is kept only if it removes them and keeps every real fact.

  • It runs on redesigns, concept pages and every page of a full site.
  • The score is saved with each version, so you can see it.
  • Writing suggestions that sound machine-made are dropped before you see them.
  1. The pageWhat ships
  2. Copy checkNo hype words, no invented proof
  3. Layout checkNo template skeletons
  4. Colour checkNo decorative purple gradients
  5. Their brandAlways exempt

The client’s brand always wins.A purple brand stays purple.

The check only flags what nobody chose. Their own colours, their own fonts and their own logo are exempt, every time.
