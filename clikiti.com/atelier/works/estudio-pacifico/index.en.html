Manifesto

Sheet A-02

We draw from the coast. Pacific light, wind and sand decide where a wall opens, how far a shadow reaches and which materials deserve to grow old beside the sea. Concrete, timber and lime, measured with the same calm you bring to a drawing, so every space feels as if it had always been there.
