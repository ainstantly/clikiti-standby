First, we listen to the engine

Before we touch a single map, we make sure the engine is healthy. A car with leaks or worn plugs doesn’t gain power; it gains problems. That’s why every job starts with an hour of diagnostics and a baseline run on the dyno.

4thBrakes & suspension

More power needs more brakes

Adding 40 horsepower, then coming down off Pichincha on factory pads, is not a plan. We bring the rest of the car up to its new engine: it stops shorter, grips harder and reaches the last corner in one piece.

  • Larger rotors
  • 4- and 6-piston calipers
  • DOT 5.1 fluid
  • Adjustable coilovers
  • Anti-roll bars
  • Alignment and corner weighting
