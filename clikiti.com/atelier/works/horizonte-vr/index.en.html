How it feels

The best part starts when the headset comes off.

Kids walk out talking about whales. Parents walk out speechless.

Every world pairs 4K headsets with wind, vibration, temperature and surround sound. Your body forgets it's in Guayaquil — which is exactly the point.

  • 01A guide in every room, always within sight.
  • 02Headsets sanitized with UV light after every jump.
  • 03A viewing deck with screens: parents watch live what their kids are seeing.
  • 04No experience runs past 20 minutes. All thrill, no motion sickness.

What we hear most in the rooms: Again!Mom, look!I'm flying!Don't let go!One more!We won!

You come in a visitor.
You leave with a story.
