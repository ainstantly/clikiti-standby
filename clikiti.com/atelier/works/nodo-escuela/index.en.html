01Curiosity

It all starts with a question: how does my phone know that face is mine? At Nodo we turn that curiosity into foundations, foundations into projects, and projects into something you present to a packed auditorium.

Nobody memorizes definitions here. You build a robot, train a model, break it, work out why and try again.

With twelve people per group at most, nobody hides in the back row and nobody gets left behind.
